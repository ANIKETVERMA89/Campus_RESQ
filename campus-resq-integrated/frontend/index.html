How routing works

Step cost = α·distance + β·risk, charged on the room being entered (the backend passes congestion as 0). Blocked rooms are skipped. Dijkstra minimises that cost; BFS minimises hops and reports distance as its cost.

Exits are the rooms whose name contains “Exit” (backend rule): Exit, Exit 4, Exit, Exit. Corridor weights come from ground.h (1 unit = 10 m). Room positions are schematic.

    Nothing dispatched yet.

    Fire and security block their room and spread risk to nearby rooms (×0.6 and ×0.5 per hop); risk from several incidents adds up. Medical adds no hazard. The queue is the backend’s priority queue: highest severity first; equal severities are not first-come-first-served.

    Evacuation plan

    Every room sends its occupants to the exit the backend picks for it (Dijkstra, evacuation weights). The backend has no crowd model, so routes do not adapt to each other.
